## Direction contract

THESIS: The live Grădina Kasper homepage is the visual authority: a full-viewport greenhouse hero establishes provenance first, followed by a wide featured box and a direct editorial article grid.

OWN-WORLD: White and pale green fields, deep green type and actions, large rounded photo and product planes, restrained secondary green, and almost no borders. Real CMS photography and logos only; no doodles, stickers, decorative badges, synthetic people, gratuitous pills, or AI-looking ornament.

STORY: Visitors understand the boxes, meet the growers, trust the greenhouse origin, and add the featured box or browse the catalogue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navigation overlays real greenhouse photography; a measured dark-green headline, short supporting copy, and one catalogue action sit inside a soft neutral light wash.

FORM: Refined live storefront, seed `bbe278cf`: preserve the current public site's viewport-filling hero and stacked section rhythm while improving mobile readability, spacing, and neutral structural styling.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
