# Homepage

- Scope: `src/app/(frontend)/page.tsx`
- Visitor mode: Persuade
- Audience: households choosing one-off vegetable boxes
- Job: understand the offer, trust its greenhouse origin, and add the featured box or browse all boxes
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/homepage-a.webp`
- Constraint: preserve routes, content ownership, business logic, and real CMS media

## Direction contract

THESIS: A balanced human-greenhouse and featured-box split makes the weekly offer immediate; it refuses the generic stacked hero followed by interchangeable cards.

OWN-WORLD: White and pale green fields, deep green type and actions, large rounded photo and product planes, restrained secondary green, and almost no borders. Real CMS photography and logos only; no doodles, stickers, decorative badges, synthetic people, gratuitous pills, or AI-looking ornament.

STORY: Visitors understand the boxes, meet the growers, trust the greenhouse origin, and add the featured box or browse the catalogue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation above a balanced split: real hero media with headline on the left; pale featured-product plane with image, price, weight, date, and primary action on the right; articles begin at the fold.

FORM: Friendly Weekly Harvest, grounded candidate 3, seed `bbe278cf`; Balanced Split comp approved.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
